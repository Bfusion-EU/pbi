# Insights pack — Home becomes a command center

Date: 2026-10-31 · Status: **revision 2 approved** (mockup v4, "one card per subject area") ·
Owner: BFusion

Revision 1 (mockup v3, "modern bento") was built and pushed in PR #3. Seen in Desktop it read as
messy: each subject showed up in two or three places (KPI row, area tile, briefing), five card
styles were mixed, the text was centred, link chips floated, and a big rounded Act-now panel
dominated the page. Revision 2 reorganises the page **by subject area**: each subject appears
once, in one uniform card.

## Goal

Home is a **command center**: one page that says what needs attention, lets the reader act on it,
and still navigates to every page of the pack. Two audiences share the page through a view switch:

- **PMO view** (weekly triage): subject-area health plus the Act-now list and the next 30 days.
- **Executive view** (monthly): the same subject-area health plus the three decisions to take.

Out of scope: model changes (all new measures are report-level, as in the rest of the pack), custom
visuals, row-level security and a mobile layout.

## Design rules

1. **One subject, one place.** A number lives on its subject card only. There is no separate KPI
   row, and the briefing strip only counts reasons.
2. **One card anatomy**, identical for every subject (see section 4).
3. **Left-aligned reading line.** Titles, numbers and sentences are left-aligned. Nothing floats.
4. **The views differ in one column only.** The subject grid is the same in both views; only the
   right-hand column changes (Act now ↔ decisions).

## Constraints (unchanged pack rules)

- Native Power BI visuals only. The page is generated by `Insights/_generator` (`ext_home.py`).
  Desktop edits are overwritten on rebuild.
- Report-level measures go in folder `Story\Home`, prefix `Story | Home · `. Reuse an existing story
  measure wherever one already answers the question: one definition per number.
- `build.py --check` and `dax_check.py` must pass, and the page must be looked at in Desktop
  before the work is done.

## Page and look

- Page 1800 × 1000, `FitToPage`, canvas `#141C2F`.
- Cards: fill `#1B2540`, radius 14, no border; text `#E8ECF4`, labels `#8C98B3`. **All cards use
  this single style**, except the orange briefing strip.
- Accent `#E8612A`: selected rail icon, briefing strip, view switch "on" state.
- Status colours on dark: red `#FF8A80`, amber `#F2C46B`, teal `#5DCAA5`, grey `#8C98B3` (no
  status). Pill fills: `#3A1E22` / `#3A2F18` / `#173A33` / `#26314F`.
- Fonts: Segoe UI (Semibold for numbers and titles), sentence case. Card number 28 pt, sentence
  11 pt, labels 10 pt.

### 1 · Navigation rail (both views, unchanged from rev 1)

- A 72 px panel at x 16 with nine 52 × 52 icon buttons: Command center (current, orange),
  1 Executive, 2 Portfolio, 3 Financials, 4 Resources, 5 Schedule, 6 Risk, 7 Benefits, 8 Governance.
- Each icon is an image visual with a transparent page-navigation button over it. The tooltip names
  the chapter and its follow-on pages. The 18 rail PNGs are already in the generator assets.

### 2 · Top bar (unchanged from rev 1, with one change)

- Title "Command center", then the greeting (`Story | Home · Greeting`) and the refresh line
  (`Story | Home · Refreshed`).
- Portfolio and Program dropdown slicers, **synced report-wide**.
- PMO / Executive switch: bookmark buttons, with an orange pill marking the active view.

### 3 · Briefing strip (both views)

- One orange strip, full width (x 104–1784), 56 high.
- Left: `Story | Home · Briefing` without its reason breakdown, as a short sentence: "11 of 15 active
  projects need a decision".
- Right: `Story | Home · Briefing Reasons`, one line listing the non-zero reason buckets:
  "1 over budget · 2 late · 5 overdue work · 3 failing rules". The buckets come from the same
  `Act Score` as the Act-now list, so they always sum to N.
- No link: the Act-now list is always visible in the PMO view.

### 4 · Subject cards (both views): the one card anatomy

Every card has the same five rows, top to bottom, all left-aligned:

1. **Header**: subject icon (small grey PNG, from the rail set), subject name, and a **status
   pill** on the right showing "Act" (red), "Watch" (amber), "OK" (teal) or "–" (grey). The pill
   text and colour come from the subject's Home colour measure.
2. **Number**: one big KPI (28 pt) in the status colour, with its label under it (10 pt, grey).
3. **Trend or bar**: a 6-month sparkline where history exists. Otherwise a progress bar where the
   KPI is a share. Otherwise this row is left empty, so the rows still line up across cards.
4. **Sentence**: one 11 pt sentence (`Story | Home · Insight <Subject>`), at most two lines,
   saying why the number is what it is.
5. **Footer**: text links to the chapter and its follow-on pages. The whole card also has a
   transparent link to the chapter.

- The pill is a new `Story | Home · Pill <Subject>` text measure that maps the colour measure:
  red → "Act", amber → "Watch", teal → "OK", anything else → "–". It is shown in a small card
  whose background is the matching pill fill (colour measure).
  Implementation note: grey (the Resources "under 50%" colour) maps to "Low".
  Implementation note: `Home · Colour Status` is red when worsened ≥ 3 or not reporting ≥ 5, amber when at least one of either is ≥ 1, else teal.
  Implementation note: the Financials card has its own count-based `Home · Colour Financials` (red if any project > 5% over EAC, amber if any project is over budget, else teal), so the number, pill and sparkline agree. `Colour EAC` stays for the Executive Money decision.
  Implementation note: the Status reports card number is "not reporting" (30+ days); the Insight sentence describes the worsened count.
  Implementation note: the Resources footer has a "Sheets" chip to the Timesheet Status page, which keeps that page reachable from Home.
  Implementation note: Benefits shows the model's 0.00 ratio format, and `Colour Benefits` is grey (pill "–") when the ratio is blank; the Insight sentence omits the EAC clause when EAC is blank.
- Timesheets no longer has its own card. It moves into the Resources sentence and links (Time,
  and TS status through the rail tooltip of chapter 4).
- **Left alignment** requires the newer native "Card (new)" visual (`cardVisual`), because the
  legacy card always centres its text. Implementation starts with a Desktop spike to check that a
  generated `cardVisual` renders, with left-aligned value and label. If it does not render, fall
  back to the legacy card for the number and accept centred text there, and say so before going
  on.

- **Implementation note:** Card (new) did not wrap sentences, so values and sentences use the one-cell table route.

### 5 · Right column, PMO view: Act now

- Title "Act now", subtitle "top 8, ranked by impact".
- Table with Project, Why and a narrow Impact column (sort key), descending. Up to 8 whole rows
  are visible. The Why text is coloured by `Act Colour`.
  Implementation note: the list is not capped at 8 — a TopN visual filter on the extension measure rendered an empty table in Desktop, so the table scrolls and is ranked by impact.
- The ranking rules are unchanged from rev 1 (first match wins): off track 100 · EAC > 5% over
  budget 90 · > 10 d later than baseline 80 · > 10 overdue tasks 60 · no status report > 30 d 50
  · failing Plan Insights rules 40. Projects with no reason are hidden.
- "Open One-Pager ›" drill-through button under the table (enabled when a row is selected), plus
  the hint "or right-click › Drill through".
- **Next 30 days**: milestones due in the next 30 days, with Due (`Story | Home · Due`,
  "dd mmm yyyy"), Project and Milestone.

### 6 · Right column, Executive view: three decisions

- Three stacked cards (Money, People, Risk) in the same card style, each with a label, the
  decision sentence (`Story | Exec Action Money / People / Risk`, left-aligned) and a footer link
  to Financials, Resources or Risk.
- The "four answers" of rev 1 are dropped: the subject cards already answer those questions.

### 7 · View switch (bookmarks)

- Only the right column is grouped: `grp_pmo` (Act now and next 30 days, plus the PMO pill) and
  `grp_exec` (three decisions, plus the Executive pill). The subject grid and briefing are not
  grouped and never change.
- Two display-only bookmarks (`suppressData`): `Home · PMO view` and `Home · Executive view`.
  The default is PMO.
- The bookmark JSON format is the one from rev 1. It has not yet been click-tested in Desktop, so
  that test is part of the acceptance checks.

## History rules (unchanged from rev 1)

- Sparklines and change chips use status-report snapshots: per month, each **active** project's
  latest report as of month end (carry forward). The window is the last 6 months.
- A change chip is blank, not "0", when there is no previous value. Do not use the model's
  `Status Report | … Previous …` measures.

## Measures

- **New** (`Story | Home · …`): `Briefing Reasons`, `Insight Financials / Schedule / Resources /
  Risk / Portfolio / Benefits / Status / Governance`, `Pill <Subject>` (8), `Colour Status`,
  and `Pill Fill <Subject>` if the pill background needs its own colour measure.
- **Changed**: `Briefing` drops its reason breakdown (that moves to `Briefing Reasons`).
- **Removed** once nothing uses them: `Line …` (8), `Answer …` (4), and the rev-1 KPI-row
  arrangement in `build_pulse`. Measures and helpers no longer used by Home are deleted.
- **Reused**: everything named in the card table, the Act-now and history measures, `Due`,
  `Greeting` and `Refreshed`.

## Testing / acceptance

- `build.py --check` OK (schema including bookmarks, references, layout within 1800 × 1000).
- `dax_check.py` 0 errors. Spot checks: the briefing reason counts sum to N; Market Research is
  in Act now; each pill shows the word that matches its colour.
- Tests: 8 subject cards, each with the five rows present in order and left-aligned; each card's
  footer and whole-card link target the right pages; every story page is reachable from Home;
  only right-column visuals belong to the groups; z-order and bookmark maps as in rev 1.
- Desktop look: both views render with nothing cut off, and the cards line up row by row.
- **Manual click checks (user)**: the view switch toggles the right column; a slicer pick survives
  the switch and carries into a chapter; rail icons, card links and footers land on the right
  pages; "Open One-Pager ›" opens the selected project.

## Risks / open points

- `cardVisual` generation is new for the generator, so it gets a spike first, with the legacy
  card as fallback.
- Status-report history is thin in the demo tenant, so the sparklines may show only a few points.
- The bookmark switch is still not click-tested (carried over from rev 1).
- Closed dropdown slicers stay white: Desktop ignores the colours for that state (known limit).
