# Insights pack — Home becomes a command center

Date: 2026-10-31 · Status: approved design (mockup v3, "modern bento") · Owner: BFusion

## Goal

Replace the Home "Story Navigator" (today: 6 pulse cards + 8 chapter tiles with buttons) with a
**command center**: one page that says what needs attention, lets the reader act on it, and still
navigates to every page of the pack. Two audiences share the page through a view switch:

- **PMO view** (weekly triage) — what to act on this week, area health, next 30 days.
- **Executive view** (monthly) — the three decisions to take, and the board's four questions answered.

Out of scope: model changes (all new measures are report-level, as in the rest of the pack), custom
visuals, row-level security, a mobile layout.

## Constraints (unchanged pack rules)

- Native Power BI visuals only; generated by `Insights/_generator` (new module `ext_home.py`
  replaces `pages.build_home`). Desktop edits are overwritten on rebuild.
- Report-level measures in folder `Story\Home`, prefix `Story | Home · `. Reuse existing story
  measures wherever one already answers the question — one definition per number.
- `build.py --check` and `dax_check.py` must pass; page viewed in Desktop before done.

## Page and look

- Page 1800 × 1000, `FitToPage`, canvas `#141C2F` (darker than today's `#1E2A44`).
- Cards: fill `#1B2540`, radius 14, no border; inner text `#E8ECF4`, labels `#8C98B3`.
- Accent `#E8612A` (selected rail icon, briefing card, view switch "on" state).
- Status colours on dark: red `#FF8A80`, amber `#F2C46B`, teal `#5DCAA5`; change-chip fills
  `#3A1E22` / `#3A2F18` / `#173A33`.
- Area tiles carry the status colour as a 3 px **top** stripe (a thin shape above the card).
- Fonts: Segoe UI (Semibold for numbers and titles), sentence case, numbers 28–32 pt.

### 1 · Navigation rail (both views, always visible)

- 72 px wide rounded panel `#1B2540`, x 16, full height. Nine 52 × 52 icon buttons, one per
  destination: Command center (current, orange fill), 1 Executive, 2 Portfolio, 3 Financials,
  4 Resources, 5 Schedule, 6 Risk, 7 Benefits, 8 Governance.
- Each icon is a native action button (Page navigation) with a custom PNG icon (white / grey on
  navy, from `StaticResources`). Button tooltip names the chapter **and its follow-on pages**
  (e.g. "5 Schedule — Gantt · Status trend · Plan vs baseline").
- Follow-on pages are reached from the area tiles (section 4) and from each chapter's own
  Next-chapter flow. Native PBI has no hover fly-out menu; the tooltip is the substitute.
- Icons: 9 PNGs, 2 states (default `#8C98B3`, selected white on orange), 48 px, rendered once from
  Tabler outline SVGs by a small script in the generator and committed as resources.

### 2 · Top bar

- Greeting (DAX): "Good morning / afternoon / evening" by `HOUR ( NOW () )` + " · PMO view" or
  " · Executive view" (static text per view group). Second line: weekday + date and
  `Story | Refreshed`.
- Portfolio and Program dropdown slicers, styled for dark, **synced report-wide** (same sync
  groups as the chapter pages), so a pick here carries into every chapter.
- View switch: two pill buttons (PMO / Executive) → bookmarks (section 6).

### 3 · Pulse (both views)

- **Briefing card** (orange, 2 cols wide): new measure `Story | Home · Briefing` — one sentence:
  "N of M active projects need a decision — a over budget, b slipping, c people above 100% in
  <next month>." N = count of projects in the Act-now list (section 4). Footer link
  "See the act-now list ›" = bookmark to the PMO view.
- **Two lead KPI cards with sparkline**: `Story | EAC Variance %` (colour `Story | Variance Colour`)
  and `Story | On Track %`. Sparkline = small line chart, no axes/legend, last 6 months:
  - EAC variance by month from FactStatusReport snapshots (Forecast vs Budget per report month),
  - On-track % by month from FactStatusReport OverallHealth per report month.
  Change chip = this month vs previous report month (new `… Change` text measures).
- **Four small KPI cards**: Active projects (`Story | Active Projects`), Utilisation
  (`Story | Utilisation %` + progress bar), Critical risks (`Story | Critical High Risks`; no chip - risk heat has
  no history), Data trust (`PMO | Governance Score` + progress bar).
  The progress bar is a 100% stacked bar chart (the value and its remainder to 100%), because a
  shape cannot be bound to data.
- Every KPI card has a transparent page-navigation button over it to its chapter.
- **Change chips only where history exists** (status-report snapshots, FactCapacity months).
  No chip is shown when the previous value is blank. Do **not** reuse the model's
  `Status Report | … Previous …` measures (they use DATEADD −1 day and are wrong); build on the
  report-to-report logic already in `ext_status.py` (Latest / Previous Report End).

### 4 · PMO view body

Left column (x 104–930): **Act now**

- Table, one row per project, max 8 rows, sorted by `Story | Home · Act Score` desc; columns:
  status dot (conditional-format icon/colour), Project, Why (worst reason), Owner (manager).
- Reason rules, first match wins, score = severity weight:
  1. Overall health Off Track (`(3)`) — 100
  2. EAC over budget > 5% (`Story | EAC Variance %`) — 90
  3. Finish > 10 days later than current baseline (`Story | Baseline · Finish Slip (days)`) — 80
  4. More than 10 overdue tasks (`Story | Project · Overdue Tasks`) — 60
  5. No status report for > 30 days (`Story | Status · Days Since Report`) — 50
  6. Failing Plan Insights rules (`Story | Project · Failing Rules` > 0) — 40
  Rows with no reason are hidden (score blank).
- Actions: right-click › Drill through › Project One-Pager; plus a button under the table
  "Open One-Pager ›" with action *Drill through* (enabled when one row is selected).
- Under it: **Next 30 days** — open milestones due in the next 30 days (existing
  milestone table pattern), 4 rows, date + project + milestone.

Right column (x 946–1784): **Area health**, 2 × 4 tiles

| Tile | Key line (measure) | Colour | Links |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portfolio | aligned budget % (`Story | Aligned Budget %`) | teal ≥ 80%, amber ≥ 60%, else red | Portfolio · Gates · Project |
| Financials | "n over EAC" (`Story | Projects Over Budget`) | `Story | Variance Colour` | Spend · Pareto |
| Resources | "n over 100%" (`Story | Over-allocated People`) | `Story | Util Colour` | People · Time · Person |
| Schedule | "n slipping" (`Story | Baseline · Late Projects`) | red > 0 slipping by > 10 d, amber > 0, else teal | Schedule · Gantt · Baseline |
| Risk | "n critical" (`Story | Critical High Risks`) | red if > 0, else teal | Risk |
| Benefits | benefit/cost ratio (`Story | Benefit Cost Ratio`) | teal ≥ 1, else amber | Benefits |
| Timesheets | "n missing" (missing-timesheet count already defined in `ext_tsstatus.py`) | amber if > 0 | TS status |
| Governance | score (`PMO | Governance Score`) | `PMO | Governance RAG Colour` | Governance |

Each tile: label, key line (new short `Story | Home · <Area> Line` text measures), status stripe
(colour measure), and 1–3 small text-link buttons (page navigation).

### 5 · Executive view body

- **Three decision cards** (x 104–1784, 3 columns): Money / People / Risk = existing
  `Story | Exec Action Money / People / Risk`; top stripe coloured by the matching colour measure;
  link to Financials / Resources / Risk.
- **Four answers** (row below): "Are we spending as planned?", "Will we deliver on time?",
  "Do we have the people?", "Will it pay off?" — one line each from the chapter headline measures
  shortened to their first sentence (new `Story | Home · Answer <Area>` measures), status stripe,
  "Open ›" link.

### 6 · View switch (bookmarks)

- Visuals of each view body are grouped (`visualGroup` containers: `grp_pmo`, `grp_exec`).
- Two bookmarks: `Home · PMO view` (pmo visible, exec hidden) and `Home · Executive view`.
  Bookmarks capture **display only** (not data, not current page), so slicer picks survive the
  switch.
- Default when the report opens: PMO view.
- Generator work: emit `definition/bookmarks/bookmarks.json` + one `*.bookmark.json` per
  bookmark, validated against the PBIR bookmark schema in `--check`.

## New measures (report-level, `Story\Home`)

Briefing · Act Score · Act Reason · Act Colour · Area lines (8) · Answers (4) ·
EAC Variance by report month · On Track by report month · Change chips (EAC, On track, Critical
risks) · Greeting. All others are reused.

## Testing / acceptance

- `build.py --check` OK (schema incl. bookmarks, references, layout inside 1800 × 1000).
- `dax_check.py` 0 errors; spot-check Act-now ranking against the demo tenant (Market Research
  +95 d must rank; a project with no reason must not appear).
- Desktop: both views render, switch works, slicer pick survives the switch and carries into a
  chapter, every rail icon and tile link lands on the right page, drill-through to One-Pager works
  from the Act-now table.
- Change chips blank (not "0") where no previous value exists.

## Risks / open points

- Status-report history is thin in the demo tenant; sparklines may show only a few points.
- Native card visuals cannot place a chip next to the number; chips are separate small cards.
- Bookmark JSON is new for the generator — validate early (first implementation task).
