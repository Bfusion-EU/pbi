# Insights pack — Home becomes a command center

Date: 2026-10-31 · Status: **revision 2 approved** (mockup v4, "one card per subject area") ·
Owner: BFusion

Revision 1 (mockup v3, "modern bento") was built and pushed in PR #3. Seen in Desktop it read as
messy: each subject showed up in two or three places (KPI row, area tile, briefing), five card
styles were mixed, the text was centred, link chips floated, and a big rounded Act-now panel
dominated the page. Revision 2 reorganises the page **by subject area**: each subject appears
once, in one uniform card.

## Goal

Home is a **command center**: one page that says what needs attention, lets the reader act on it,
and still navigates to every page of the pack. Two audiences share the page through a view switch:

- **PMO view** (weekly triage): subject-area health plus the Act-now list and the next 30 days.
- **Executive view** (monthly): the same subject-area health plus the three decisions to take.

Out of scope: model changes (all new measures are report-level, as in the rest of the pack), custom
visuals, row-level security and a mobile layout.

## Design rules

1. **One subject, one place.** A number lives on its subject card only. There is no separate KPI
   row, and the header chips only count reasons.
2. **One card anatomy**, identical for every subject (see section 4).
3. **Left-aligned reading line.** Titles, numbers and sentences are left-aligned. Nothing floats.
4. **The views differ in one column only.** The subject grid is the same in both views; only the
   right-hand column changes (Act now ↔ decisions).

## Constraints (unchanged pack rules)

- Native Power BI visuals only. The page is generated by `Insights/_generator` (`ext_home.py`).
  Desktop edits are overwritten on rebuild.
- Report-level measures go in folder `Story\Home`, prefix `Story | Home · `. Reuse an existing story
  measure wherever one already answers the question: one definition per number.
- `build.py --check` and `dax_check.py` must pass, and the page must be looked at in Desktop
  before the work is done.

## Page and look

- Page 1800 × 1000, `FitToPage`, canvas `#141C2F`.
- Cards: fill `#1B2540`, radius 14, no border; text `#E8ECF4`, labels `#8C98B3`. **All cards use
  this single style**. The header band uses the same panel colour.
- Accent `#E8612A`: selected rail icon, the decision pill, view switch "on" state.
- Status colours on dark: red `#FF8A80`, amber `#F2C46B`, teal `#5DCAA5`, grey `#8C98B3` (no
  status). Pill fills: `#3A1E22` / `#3A2F18` / `#173A33` / `#26314F`.
- Fonts: Segoe UI (Semibold for numbers and titles), sentence case.
- Sizes (rev 2.1, enlarged for readability): card title 14 pt Semibold, card icon 28 px, status
  pill 11 pt (box 72 x 28), card number 34 pt, number label 12 pt, sentence 13 pt (up to four
  lines in the Service), footer link chips 11 pt (30 high; each chip is sized to its text, 8 px per character + 22 px,
  laid out left to right with a 10 px gap; the arrow is dropped, then the font goes to 10 pt, when a card's chips do not fit). Right column: "Act now" / "Next 30 days" titles 15 pt,
  table header and body 11 pt, hint 10 pt, decision labels and decision text 13 pt. Header
  band: decision pill 15 pt, reason chips 12 pt, band 76 high (y 8-84).
- Cell headroom: the Service renders text taller than Desktop and shows a scroll thumb when a one-cell table
  is too short, and the table header row (about 22 px) counts. Every number / sentence cell is therefore at least
  28 px taller than header + text. Card rows (y relative to the card top): head 12, number cell 40 (120 high, its
  empty lower part sits under the label), label 120, row 3 (spark / bar) 160-256, sentence cell 256 (126 high,
  drawn before the spark / bar so they sit on top of its empty upper part), rule 386 (row height 436 minus 50),
  chips 394 (30 high, chip padding 24 px, gap 8 px).

### 3 · Decision pill and reason chips (both views, inside the band)

- **Pill**: orange `#E8612A` rounded shape (radius 10) at x 436, y 20, 270 x 52, with `Story | Home · Briefing Pill`
  centred in white, 15 pt: "11 of 15 need a decision", or "No project needs a decision" when N = 0.
- **Six fixed chips** after the pill (x 720 on), in two rows of three (y 14 and y 50, 28 high, gap 8), one per
  Act-score bucket in this order: off track (100), over budget (90), late (80), overdue work (60), not reporting (50),
  failing rules (40). Each chip is a rounded outline shape (`#3A4768` line, transparent fill), a small dot (8 px: red
  `#FF8A80` for off track, over budget and late; amber `#F2C46B` for the other three) and a single-line text,
  12 pt, `Story | Home · Chip <Bucket>` ("1 over budget", "0 over budget" when zero). The text colour is
  `Chip Colour <Bucket>`: `#E8ECF4` when the count is above 0, otherwise dim `#5A6680`. Chip width = dot zone + 7.5 px
  per character of the widest label ("00 " + label) + padding: 128, 143, 90.5 / 150.5, 158, 158. The chips come from
  the same `Act Score` buckets as the Act-now list, so the six counts always add up to the pill's N (`Act Count`).
- Pill and chip texts are card visuals (transparent background, no header, no scroll), not one-cell tables: the
  table header row (about 26 px) leaves no room in a 28 px chip, and its background would paint outside the band.
- Hover and click (rev 2.3): hovering the pill or a chip lists the projects behind it; clicking the count part of a
  chip filters the Act-now column to that reason, clicking the count part of the pill shows all again. See section 8.

### 4 · Subject cards (both views): the one card anatomy

Every card has the same five rows, top to bottom, all left-aligned:

1. **Header**: subject icon (small grey PNG, from the rail set), subject name, and a **status
   pill** on the right showing "Act" (red), "Watch" (amber), "OK" (teal) or "–" (grey). The pill
   text and colour come from the subject's Home colour measure.
2. **Number**: one big KPI (34 pt) in the status colour, with its label under it (12 pt, grey).
3. **Trend or bar**: a 6-month sparkline where history exists. Otherwise a progress bar where the
   KPI is a share. The bar is ONE stacked `barChart` with two measures, the value (status colour) and its
   remainder to the axis max (`#3A4768`: `Home · Util Rest`, `Aligned Rest`, `Trust Rest`), value axis fixed to
   0..max, axes/legend/labels/title/background hidden, category inner padding 60 (about 14 px bar). Fill and
   track are the same bars, so they cannot misalign. The chart is 110 px tall (Desktop shows a placeholder below
   ~100 px) and as wide as the track. Otherwise this row is left empty, so the rows still line up across cards.
4. **Sentence**: one 13 pt sentence (`Story | Home · Insight <Subject>`), at most three lines,
   saying why the number is what it is.
5. **Footer**: text links to the chapter and its follow-on pages. The whole card also has a
   transparent link to the chapter.

| Card | Number (measure) | Label | Row 3 | Sentence (new `Insight …` measure, built from) | Colour / pill | Footer links |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Financials | `Story | Projects Over Budget` | projects over EAC | sparkline `Home · EAC Var % (reports)` | `Story | EAC Variance %` + worst over-budget project | new `Home · Colour Financials` (red: any project > 5% over EAC; amber: any project over budget; else teal) | Spend · Pareto |
| Schedule | `Story | Baseline · Late Projects` | slipping vs baseline | sparkline `Home · On Track % (reports)` | `Story | On Track %` + `On Track Change` + worst slip | `Home · Colour Schedule` | Schedule · Gantt · Baseline |
| Resources | `Story | Utilisation %` | utilisation | progress bar (0..1) | `Story | Over-allocated People` (in view) + `Story | TS Status · Missing Timesheets` | `Home · Colour Util` | People · Time · TS · Person |
| Risk | `Story | Critical High Risks` | critical risks | — | `Story | Active Risk Count` + `Story | Unmitigated Risk %` | `Home · Colour Risk` | Risk |
| Portfolio | `Story | Aligned Budget %` | budget aligned | progress bar (0..1) | `Story | Active Projects` + `Story | Proposed Projects` | `Home · Colour Portfolio` | Gates · Scenarios · Project |
| Benefits | `Story | Benefit Cost Ratio` (format `0.00`, the model's) | benefit / cost | — | `Story | Benefits At Completion` vs `Story | Finance EAC` | `Home · Colour Benefits` (grey when no ratio) | Benefits |
| Status reports | `Story | Status · Not Reporting` | not reporting 30+ days | — | `Story | Status · Overall Worsened` (projects worse since their last report) | new `Home · Colour Status` (worsened ≥ 3 or not reporting ≥ 5 → red; ≥ 1 of either → amber; else teal) | Trend |
| Governance | `PMO | Governance Score` | data trust score | progress bar (0..100) | `PMO | Plans Failing` (plans failing Plan Insights rules) | `Home · Colour Trust` | Governance |

- The pill is a new `Story | Home · Pill <Subject>` text measure that maps the colour measure:
  red → "Act", amber → "Watch", teal → "OK", anything else → "–". It is shown in a small card
  whose background is the matching pill fill (colour measure).
  Implementation note: grey (the Resources "under 50%" colour) maps to "Low".
  Implementation note: `Home · Colour Status` is red when worsened ≥ 3 or not reporting ≥ 5, amber when at least one of either is ≥ 1, else teal.
  Implementation note: the Financials card has its own count-based `Home · Colour Financials` (red if any project > 5% over EAC, amber if any project is over budget, else teal), so the number, pill and sparkline agree. `Colour EAC` stays for the Executive Money decision.
  Implementation note: the Status reports card number is "not reporting" (30+ days); the Insight sentence describes the worsened count.
  Implementation note: the Resources footer has a "TS" chip to the Timesheet Status page, which keeps that page reachable from Home.
  Implementation note: Benefits shows the model's 0.00 ratio format, and `Colour Benefits` is grey (pill "–") when the ratio is blank; the Insight sentence omits the EAC clause when EAC is blank.
- Timesheets no longer has its own card. It moves into the Resources sentence and links (Time,
  and TS status through the rail tooltip of chapter 4).
- **Left alignment** requires the newer native "Card (new)" visual (`cardVisual`), because the
  legacy card always centres its text. Implementation starts with a Desktop spike to check that a
  generated `cardVisual` renders, with left-aligned value and label. If it does not render, fall
  back to the legacy card for the number and accept centred text there, and say so before going
  on.

- **Implementation note:** Card (new) did not wrap sentences, so values and sentences use the one-cell table route.

### 8 · What's behind this number: hover lists and click-to-filter (rev 2.3)

**Hover: report-page tooltips.** Fifteen hidden pages (page type Tooltip, 360 × 260, white, `HiddenInViewMode`,
registered in `pages.HIDDEN_PAGES` under stable keys `tip_home_*`). Each has a title, a one-line subtitle, an orange
rule and ONE light table. Every table lists rows through measures that are blank unless the row qualifies, so the
other rows vanish; there is never a visual filter on a measure (that rendered an empty table in Desktop). Column
filters (real projects, risk work type / state) are fine.

| Page | Attached to | Rows | Columns (blank-unless measure in bold) | Same rule as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tip_home_act` | pill | projects | Project, **Act Reason**, Act Score | Act Count |
| `tip_home_<bucket>` × 6 | chip text | projects | Project, **`Home · Tip <Bucket>`** = `IF ( [Act Score] = k, [Act Reason] )` | Chip <Bucket> |
| `tip_home_fin` | Financials number | projects | Project, **Tip Financials** (EAC variance %) | Projects Over Budget |
| `tip_home_sch` | Schedule number | projects | Project, **Tip Schedule** (finish slip, days) | Baseline · Late Projects |
| `tip_home_res` | Resources number | people | Person, **Tip Resources** (peak monthly utilisation > 100%, active people) | Over-allocated People |
| `tip_home_risk` | Risk number | risks (DimTask) | Risk, Project, **Tip Risk** (exposure of active risks scoring 9+) | Critical High Risks |
| `tip_home_port` | Portfolio number | projects | Project, **Tip Portfolio** (Aligned / Not aligned, "· proposed"), **Tip Portfolio Budget** | Aligned Budget % |
| `tip_home_ben` | Benefits number | projects | Project, **Tip Benefits** (benefit / cost) | Benefit Cost Ratio |
| `tip_home_sr` | Status reports number | projects | Project, **Tip Status** (Overdue / Nothing submitted), **Tip Status Days** | Status · Not Reporting |
| `tip_home_gov` | Governance number | project plans | Project, **Tip Governance** (failing rule count) | Plans Failing (project plans only) |

The tooltip is set on the visual with `visualTooltip` `{show, type "Canvas", section <page id>}`. A button cannot show
a report-page tooltip, so every visual that carries one sits ABOVE the whole-card ghost link: the card number cell is
added after the ghost link and is only 180 px wide (`NUM_W`), so the rest of its row still clicks through to the
chapter. A tooltip cannot scroll: a list longer than about eight rows shows its top rows (each list is sorted by its
figure, worst first).

**Click: the Act-now column filtered by reason.** Seven versions of the Act-now list sit in the same place, each in
its own top-level group: `grp_act_all` (Project, Why = `Act Reason`, Impact; the rev 2 table) and `grp_act_<bucket>`
× 6 (title "Act now — <bucket>", a "Show all ›" bookmark button, and a table with Project and Why = that bucket's
`Tip <Bucket>` measure, so only that bucket's projects show). The rest of the PMO column is `grp_pmo_rest`.

| Bookmark (display only, `suppressData`) | Visible | Hidden |
|---|---|---|
| Home · PMO view (default) | pmo_rest, act_all | act buckets, exec |
| Home · Executive view | exec | everything else |
| Home · Act now — <bucket> × 6 | pmo_rest, act_<bucket> | act_all, other buckets, exec |

**Hover and click on the same chip.** A data visual on top is needed for the hover list, a button on top for the
click, and Power BI passes neither through the other. Each chip is therefore split: the LEFT zone (dot and count,
47 px, `CHIP_HIT_W`) is a transparent bookmark button above the text with a faint white tint on hover, so it reads
as clickable; the rest of the chip (the label words) is the text card, which shows the list on hover. The pill works
the same way: its left 100 px (the "11 of 15" part of the centred text) is a button back to the PMO view (all
projects), the rest hovers. The PMO switch also returns to the PMO view, and each bucket view has "Show all ›".
Alternatives rejected: a button over the whole chip (no hover list at all), the text on top of a full-size button
(only the 2-4 px rims would click).


## History rules (unchanged from rev 1)

- Sparklines and change chips use status-report snapshots: per month, each **active** project's
  latest report as of month end (carry forward). The window is the last 6 months.
- A change chip is blank, not "0", when there is no previous value. Do not use the model's
  `Status Report | … Previous …` measures.

## Measures

- **New** (`Story | Home · …`): `Briefing Pill`, `Chip <Bucket>` (6) and `Chip Colour <Bucket>` (6), `Insight Financials / Schedule / Resources /
  Risk / Portfolio / Benefits / Status / Governance`, `Pill <Subject>` (8), `Colour Status`,
  and `Pill Fill <Subject>` if the pill background needs its own colour measure.

  `Tip Governance`: each blank unless the row qualifies (section 8).

## Testing / acceptance

- `build.py --check` OK (schema including bookmarks, references, layout within 1800 × 1000).
- `dax_check.py` 0 errors. Spot checks: the six chip counts sum to N (the pill); Market Research is
  in Act now; each pill shows the word that matches its colour.
- Tests: 8 subject cards, each with the five rows present in order and left-aligned; each card's
  footer and whole-card link target the right pages; every story page is reachable from Home;
  only right-column visuals belong to the groups; z-order and bookmark maps as in rev 1.
- Desktop look: both views render with nothing cut off, and the cards line up row by row.
- **Manual click checks (user)**: the view switch toggles the right column; a slicer pick survives
  the switch and carries into a chapter; rail icons, card links and footers land on the right
  pages; "Open One-Pager ›" opens the selected project.
- **Manual hover / click checks (user, rev 2.3)**: hovering the pill, each chip's label and each card number shows
  its list; clicking a chip's count shows "Act now — <bucket>" with only those projects; "Show all ›", the pill's
  count and the PMO switch bring the full list back; the rest of each card still opens its chapter.

## Risks / open points

- `cardVisual` generation is new for the generator, so it gets a spike first, with the legacy
  card as fallback.
- Status-report history is thin in the demo tenant, so the sparklines may show only a few points.
- The bookmark switch is still not click-tested (carried over from rev 1).
- Closed dropdown slicers stay white: Desktop ignores the colours for that state (known limit).
